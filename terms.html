Some Frequently Used Dermatology Terms

  • Acne: pimples, blackheads, and whiteheads
  • Eczema: dry, itchy, and inflamed skin.
  • Psoriasis: autoimmune disease that causes thick, scaly skin patches
  • Rosacea: facial redness and visible blood vessels
  • Dermatitis: inflammation or irritation of the skin
  • Melanoma: type of skin cancer that develops in pigment-producing cells
  • Basal Cell Carcinoma: type of skin cancer, usually caused by sun exposure
  • Hives: Raised, itchy welts on the skin caused by an allergic reaction
  • Wart: small, rough skin growth caused by a virus
  • Vitiligo: condition that causes patches of skin to lose their color
  • Mole: small, usually harmless cluster of pigmented skin cells
  • Skin Tag:small, soft, noncancerous growth that hangs from the skin
  • Cyst: closed sac beneath the skin filled with fluid, air, or other material
  • Fungal Infection: skin infection caused by fungi, often leading to itching or a rash
